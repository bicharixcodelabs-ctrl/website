Process

How an engagement runs

A straight line from first call to shipped software, with no handoffs between departments you'll never meet.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We map the problem, the users, and the constraints before a single screen gets designed.

  2. 02

    Design

    Architecture and interface decisions get made together, so the build doesn't fight the design later.

  3. 03

    Build

    Short cycles, visible progress, and a staging link you can poke at from week one.

  4. 04

    Launch & support

    We ship it, watch it, and stay reachable for the fixes and iterations that come after launch.
